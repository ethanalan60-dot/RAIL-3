# AI assistance and author responsibility

AI-assisted tools, including ChatGPT and Codex (OpenAI), were used to support code development and debugging, preparation of reproducible visualization scripts, and manuscript drafting, organization, and language editing. These tools provided suggestions and implementation support under the authors' direction. The authors determined and approved the experimental design, made the final methodological and analytical decisions, and approved the interpretation and conclusions. The authors retain full responsibility for the correctness, integrity, and reproducibility of the work.

This statement reflects confirmation supplied by the user on behalf of the
author group. It does not assert separate signatures, independent verification
by every author, a particular language-model version or a retrospective
approval timestamp. Recorded assistance is not restricted to spelling checks.
SAM 3.1 is a separate frozen scientific backend; its segmentation outputs are
not human annotations or language-model-generated replacements.

The original research history and actual development attribution remain in
the private archive. A release snapshot is not a reconstructed human-only
development history. The authors declare no competing interests relevant to
this work. Code and third-party redistribution rights are separate questions.
